# How to Play
When you attempt something risky, the GM will ask you to make a save (STR, DEX or WIL) to avoid a consequence. Roll a d20, if the attribute meets or beats the result you successfully avoid the consequence.

If you have an applicable skill or gear the save is easier *(has reduced risk, increased reward and/or rolls with advantage).*

You can also bargain for an easier save.

# Combat
**Attacking**: Roll weapon die minus target's armor.
**Multiple Attackers**: Deal damage equal to the single highest die only.
**Order**: Everyone declares what they are doing *(including the GM)*, attacks resolve in logical order. *(e.g. in close quarters a daggers hit first, but at distance a polearm hits first)*
**Modifiers**:
- **Impaired** *(Cover, grappled)*: Roll d4 damage.
- **Bonus** *(Vulnerable foe)*: Get +d6, +d8 etc. to the roll.
**Maneuvers**: Instead of damage, inflict an effect *(trip/shove)*. Make a save.
**Armor**: Subtract from damage taken.

# Health & Damage
**Hit Protection** (HP): Damage depletes HP first *(shrugging off hits)*.
**Attribute Damage**: Once HP is 0, damage reduces STR.
**Critical Damage**: After taking STR damage, make a STR Save:
- **Success**: Keep fighting.
- **Fail**: Take Injured condition and become incapacitated. Die in 1 hour if untended.

# Recovery & Conditions
**Short Rest** *(10 min)*: Restores all HP.
**Damage** *(STR)* restores 1 point per day of rest in a safe place *(town)*.
**Intoxication** *(DEX)* often fully restores after a nights rest *(depending on the intoxicant)*.
**Stress** *(WIL)* fully recovers when you [carouse](#carousing) or spend 30g indulging some vice.
**Conditions**: Fill inventory slots. Cleared by meeting specific requirements *(often rest)*.
**Deprivation**: If lacking food/rest, you cannot recover HP/Stats and gain Fatigue daily.

# Magic Items
- **Items have Charges** (usually 3). To use, declare how many charges (dice) you are investing:
- **Effect**: Sum of all dice rolled.
- For each die:
	- 4-5: Consumes a charge.
	- 6: Explodes (roll another d6).
- If you roll:
	- Doubles: Mishap. 
	- Triples: Doom.
- **Recharge**: Requires a specific action to refresh all charges.
