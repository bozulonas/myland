# Cairn Sessions: Plot Summary (Sessions 1-6)

Here is a summary of the adventures of your party (Eon, Needle Fletchwind, Rook, Woozy, and later Beauregard) across the sea of liquid sands.

## Sessions 1 & 2: The Tomb of the Serpent Kings
The adventure begins with the party exploring the labyrinthine Tomb of the Serpent Kings. Delving into its depths, they uncover ancient lore, hidden gold via an ornate statue puzzle, and face deadly creatures. After a close call with a giant scorpion that forces them into a tactical retreat, they return to the surface only to find their ship, *The Dagger Dagger*, damaged by a sandstorm and their supplies spoiled.

## Session 3: Voyage to Kufla
Stranded in the harsh desert, the young Rook is surprisingly appointed as the ship's Captain. The crew sets sail across a sea of literal liquid sand, opting to head toward the mysterious city of Kufla instead of returning to their home in Axiom. The journey is fraught with peril:
- They harvest a colossal, dying dire cactus for sustenance.
- They discover a smooth, glass-like dome island that Rook names "Rooktopia," which violently sinks into the sand after Eon strikes it and sets off a fire egg.
- Needle Fletchwind is paralyzed by a venomous sand-dwelling sea urchin, requiring a rescue.
Surviving these trials and a blinding sandstorm, the crew successfully docks at Kufla, a bustling city carved into canyon cliffs.

## Session 4: Carousing and Consequences in the Red City
The party spends their hard-earned gold and downtime carousing in Kufla, interacting with local factions like the Thieves Guild and learning about their classes. However, trouble brews when Beauregard gets caught cheating at gambling in The Gatherers Guild. He is brought before the royal minister, branded, and given an ultimatum: the party must recover the legendary **Potion of Eternal Life** from an ancient sorcerer back in the Tomb of the Serpent Kings.

## Session 5: The Mirror Tower and the Sand Elves
Setting out to fulfill their forced mission, *The Dagger Dagger* is battered by fierce winds. The ineptly helmed ship takes shelter near a rocky outcropping housing a lighthouse-like Mirror Tower. While exploring the tower, they are ambushed by a Sand Elf war galley. 

Using flashback-inducing mushrooms, clever lockpicking, and manipulating the tower's mirrors, the crew engages in a fierce battle. They manage to defeat the elves, capture the elven captain (Khadiga), and seize control of the elven war galley—which is uniquely powered by passive Sand Golems. They also uncover a map leading to the **Staff of Seastone**, an artifact capable of turning the sand sea into glass.

![[Pasted image 20260503100526.png]]
## Session 6: The God's Blood and a Change of Course
Interrogating the captive Captain Khadiga, the party uncovers a massive secret: her holy mission is to retrieve the Staff of Seastone to disrupt Kufla's trade because Kufla has kidnapped her people's God. The party connects the dots and realizes that Kufla is bleeding this captive deity to produce the miraculous red liquid (likely related to the Potion of Life they were sent to find).

Armed with this revelation, the crew abandons their mission to return to the Tomb. Instead, they pivot to pursue the Staff of Seastone. Their new path leads them to a wizard's dome, where they defeat a wood-and-bone automaton and discover the body of an ancient sorcerer, setting the stage for their next confrontation.
