You've learned to ***control*** blood and bone. Command a nearby source and it adheres to your bidding
# Procedure
- **Declare Intent**: Issue a command to a nearby source of blood or bone, describe your intended effect.
- **Minimum Power**: the GM adjudicates the Power needed, see [example power](#example-powwr) below.
- **Roll Power**: Invest as much Power as you dare, up to amount available to you from items, [ritual](link to ritual achievement) or other sources. Roll that many d6. 
- What happens:
	- **Spell effect** is based on the [sum] of the roll and the number of [dice] rolled.
	- **4 & 5 Fatigue**. For each die which shows 4 or 5 gain the [fatigued condition](#fatigue).
	- **6 Explodes**. For each die which shows 6 roll another d6.
	- **Doubles** cause a [mishap](#mishaps). 
	- **Triples** cause a [Doom](#dooms)


# Additional Levels
Each time you gain a level, you can learn an additional technique which can be used in place of ***control***:
- Summon
- Perceive
- Transmute
- Destroy
- Protection from

# Example Power
Use the examples below as guidance for the minim power required for a desired effect:

| Example       | Power 1                                                     | Power 2-3                                                        | Power 4                                                                 |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Control Bone  | A femur is flung across the room like a dart ([sum] damage) | A skull hovers by your side menacingly                           | A skeleton is animated                                                  |
| Control Blood | Your touch causes bloodlust.                                | Transfer life essence between two subjects (exchange STR totals) | The target's blood leaves their hosts body, becoming a blood elemental. |

## Modifying  Power
Factors give cause for the minimum power to be increased or decreased.

Increase the minimum power if...
- Sources of blood or bone still under the possession of an unwilling target are harder to command than liberated sources.
- Range, duration or area are stretched.

Decrease the minimum power if...
- Specific sources are used to fitting effect. i.e. a giant cat's skull might be adept at hunting or the blood of a siren might serve as a aphrodisiac.

# Dooms

One way to escape this doom is to eat the heart of an immortal creature. Another way is to journey
into hell and make a bargain with the Underpope or one of the Satans.
1. You die and spend the next 1d6 days in the afterlife. You revive afterwards as long as your body is intact. Your body will rot after 2 days unless it is preserved in some way (the services of an embalmer, freezing, dessication, pickling). These preservation methods will not affect your ability to revive, rot is the only thing that will prevent it. *(Assume that all necromancers know of this incipient Doom and forewarn their companions.)*
2. You become undead, permanently.
3. All corpses in 20 miles rise as skeletons and attempt to kill you. Average = 3d20 skeletons. (For example, perhaps a nearby hill is the cairn of a long-buried skeletal giant and his two corpse mammoths. The hill just cracks open and spills out undead.) These undead were sent by someone you pissed off *(the Underpope, a Satan, a powerful psychopomp)* and will follow you to the ends of the earth. Until you destroy them, they will pursue you, and they will be joined by those that they kill.
