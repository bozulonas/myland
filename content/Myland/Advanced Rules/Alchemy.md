### Alchemy
Anyone can brew potions, powders or vapors using the procedure below but without adequate training [^1] alchemy is [hard](Core%20Rules#difficulty) [^2].

 [^1]: *such as a level in [alchemist](classes#alchemst)* 
 [^2]: *saves are rolled with disadvantage and/or mishaps use a larger die*

Procedure
1. **Harvest ingredients** from dead monsters or the environment, takes 10 min and 1 inventory slot. May contain multiple charges *(represented by usage die)*. 
2. **Brew a potion** using one or more ingredients, a cauldron, and 4 hours. Produce 1 [d100 random potion](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0BxVHEMMjLlZ4NDJqcFhYdmNVcFE/view?resourcekey=0-JpDpfFGsRDEEIxoLsKbIzg) or **brew a specific potion**, which takes 1 inventory slot *(stacks up to 3)*. Make a DEX save to avoid an **alchemical mishap**.
	1. **Brew a specific potion** using the following procedure:
		1. Player **proposes the desired effect**, using the ingredient(s) as a touchstone *(such as a Dragon's Lung producing a potion of fire breath)*. Combining multiple ingredients is cause for a more powerful effect.
		2. GM **asserts the strength of the potion** *(d4 to d12, default to d6)* based on the proposed effect *(cause to increase die size)* and how on-theme and how many ingredients are used *(cause to reduce die size).* This sets the size of the die that will be rolled if there is a alchemical mishap.
		3. Player **agrees**, produces the potion *(1 inventory slot, stacks up to 3)* and makes a WIL save to avoid an **alchemical mishap**, as normal.
	2. **Alchemical mishap**, roll on the [Alchemical Mishaps](#alchemical+mishaps) table with a d6 *(or d4 to d12 based on the potion's strength)*.
	3. **Overnight brewing** can produce 2 potions per night, but you are deprived the next day. Take a sniff of *alchemists helper*, which you always seem to have on you, to ignore this for an hour, after which make a WIL save against [addiction](#addictive+substances).
3. **Consume Potion** which takes an action.
	1. If you ever consume two potions at once, roll d6 on the [Potion Miscibility Table](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0BxVHEMMjLlZ4NDJqcFhYdmNVcFE/view?resourcekey=0-JpDpfFGsRDEEIxoLsKbIzg) *(page 11)*

#### Addictive Substances

Addictive substances last an 1 hour *(expiring when a 1 is rolled on the dungeon clock)* after which make a WIL save to resist gaining the **addicted** condition *(1 inventory slot, multiples stack)*.

> **Addicted**: You are deprived *(cannot regain HP)* unless you are on something. **Clear**: Abstain for a week & roll for **Fallout**.

##### Fallout
Roll a die based on your level of [addiction] *(d6 to d12)* and gain that fallout condition *(1 inventory slot)*.
[1] → d6 
[2] → d8
[3] → d10
[4] → d12

> **Clear:**  dangerous ritual or medical intervention
