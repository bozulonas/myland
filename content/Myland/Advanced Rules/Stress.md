
Monsters can inflict stress, which takes away from hp and then WIL

Whenever you suffer WIL damage from stress, roll a WIL save to avoid critical stress.

**Failure**: you get an affliction 
**Success**: Get a virtue

# Afflictions
**Clear**: WIL Save after long rest.

| d8  | Afflictions | Effect                                                                                 |
| --- | ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Fearful     | Hp reduced to 0, all attacks are impaired.                                             |
| 2   | Paranoid    | Cannot act in the first round, must pass a WIL Save to attack.                         |
| 3   | Selfish     | Cannot use items to help other characters.                                             |
| 4   | Masochistic | Hp reduced to 0, refuses to be healed, has advantage on critical damage saving throws. |
| 5   | Hopeless    | All stress is received double.                                                         |
| 6   | Irrational  | Must always attack the nearest enemy.                                                  |
| 7   | Abusive     | Causes 1 stress point to a random ally when acting.                                    |
| 8   | Rapturous   | Must pass a WIL Save or attack an ally each Round.                                     |

# Virtues
**Clear**: After any rest.

| d6  | Virtues    | Effect                                                      |
| --- | ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Stalwart   | Cannot receive stress points.                               |
| 2   | Courageous | +1 temp WIL for all nearby allies whenever you make a save. |
| 3   | Focused    | Advantage on all Saves.                                     |
| 4   | Powerful   | All your attacks are enhanced.                              |
| 5   | Vigorous   | +1 hp after each action in combat.                          |
| 6   | Determined | Cannot get negative Conditions.                             |
