Anyone can wield sorcery by reciting a written spell, but doing so under time pressure requires a save.
### Sorcery 
- To cast a spell, apply as much Power as you dare, up to amount available to you from items, [ritual](link to ritual achievement) or other sources (spells come with 1 power). Roll that many d6.
- What happens:
	- **Spell effect** is based on the [sum] of the roll and the number of [dice] rolled.
	- **4 & 5 Fatigue**. For each die which shows 4 or 5 gain the [fatigued condition](#fatigue).
	- **6 Explodes**. For each die which shows 6 roll another d6.
	- **Doubles** cause a [mishap](#mishaps). 
	- **Triples** cause a [Doom](#dooms)

#### Spellbooks
- **Size**: Takes 1 inventory slot.
- **Daily Study:** A wizard without access to their spellbook for study soon finds themselves unable to cast spells *(spells are hard to hold in the mind).*
- **Transcription**: Move a spell to a new spellbook  *(20g and a day of effort)*. DEX save to avoid a [spelling error](#spelling-error).
#### Scrolls
- **Scrolls** contain a single spell and a destroyed when used. 
- **Transcription**: Move a spell to a spellbook  *(20g and a day of effort)*. DEX save to avoid a [spelling error](#spelling-error).

#### Spelling Error
- The spell’s name has a few letters changed. The PCs suggest alternatives of the spell, changing a few letters from the original spelling. The GM picks from the alternatives suggested.

#### Spell Invention
- Wizard's can choose to invent instead of studying existing spells. See [Spell invention](Spell Invention)
- Invented spells can do whatever the name implies, but may have minimum Power requirements.

#### Dooms
When you roll triples experience the next of your three Dooms. Every apprentice knows that if they travel down the road of spellcasting long enough, their doom will eventually claim them. It waits at the end of every spellcaster's career.

Each school of magic has different Dooms.
1. The first time you roll triples, you suffer your first Doom.
2. The second time you roll triples, you suffer your second Doom.
3. The third time you roll triples, you suffer your third and final Doom.

While your first and second Dooms tend to be survivable, your final Doom usually spells the end of your career (usually through your death).

There are three ways to avoid your Doom.
1. Never use more than two Power at a time, forever limiting your magical power.
2. Seek out a solution to your Doom. These are always unique quests that your DM designs for you. They are usually pretty epic.
3. Sell your soul to a god or supernatural being.


#### Ritual
Draw on a place of power to create a magical effect, tell The GM what you’re trying to achieve. Ritual effects are always possible, but The GM will give you one to four of the following conditions:
- It’s going to take days/weeks/months
- First you must X
- You’ll need help from X
- It will require a lot of money
- The best you can do is a lesser version, unreliable and limited
- You and your allies will risk danger from X
- You’ll have to disenchant X to do it
