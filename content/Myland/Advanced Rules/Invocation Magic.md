### Invocation Magic
Mortals can worship gods *(and other supernatural beings)* to be granted blessings and invocations *(which cost debt)*.
#### Worship
Worship entails making a worthy [sacrifice](#sacrifice) to a god *(or supernatural being)* in exchange for their blessing, or permission to invoke their power (referred to as an invocation). When taking a level in priest you get both.
- A blessing is a minor ability.
- An invocation is like a spell.
#### Sacrifice
- A **sacrifice** is a rite requiring a worthy offering, a sacred location, and a show of humility. Grand sacrifices are needed for **Worship**, while lesser ones can clear divine debt.
- Anyone can perform a sacrifice to ask for a favor, but if you're not a priest it's [hard](Core%20Rules.md#Difficulty) *(High-risk low-reward)*
#### Invocation 
- To cast an invocation, incur as much debt as you dare, up to the maximum granted to you *(each level of Priest grants +1 maximum debt per cast)*. 
- Roll a number of d6 equal to the debt incurred. Invocations will refer number of [dice] rolled and the [sum] of the roll to determine their effect. 
- Add the debt incurred to your accumulated debt *(1 inventory slot, stacks)*.
- Rolling doubles causes [hubris](#hubris), triples causes [wrath](#wrath)
#### Debt
- Mortals are surprisingly well suited to being saddled with debt. Any amount of debt always takes 1 inventory slot *(it stacks)*
* Clear debt by performing a minor to moderate appeasement *(depending on the amount of debt)*
* At any time your god may issue a commandment, if you do not immediately fulfill it take [debt]d6 WIL damage. If you do fulfill it clear your debt.
