
### Vancian Magic

- Anyone can cast spells from spell books or scrolls.
- To cast a spell, invest as much Power into it as you dare, up to amount available to you from items, [ritual](link to ritual achievement) or other sources. Roll that many d6.
- What happens:
	- **Spell effect** is based on the [sum] of the roll and the number of [dice] rolled.
	- **4 & 5 Fatigue**. For each die which shows 4 or 5 gain the [fatigued condition](#fatigue).
	- **6 Explodes**. For each die which shows 6 roll another d6.
	- **Doubles** cause a [mishap](#mishaps). 
	- **Triples** cause a [Doom](#dooms)

#### Spell books
- **Spell books** take one inventory slot. It takes 10 min of gesticulation and incantation to cast from a spellbook (1 roll of the dungeon clock). Casting faster *(in combat)* is possible, but requires a WIL save to avoid a [mishap](#mishap).
- Spell books are considered dangerous to display openly.

#### Scrolls
- **Scrolls** contain a single spell and a destroyed when used. The spell they're in can be copied to a spell book taking 20 gold and one day. DEX save to avoid a [spelling error](#spelling-error)

#### Spelling Error
- The spell’s effect is determined by changing a few letters of the spell. The PCs suggest alternatives of the spell, changing a few letters from the original spelling. The GM picks from the alternatives suggested and describes how this magic effect is realized in the situation.

#### Spell Invention
- Wizard's can choose to invent instead of studying existing spells. See [Spell invention](Spell Invention)
- Invented spells can do whatever the name implies, but may have minimum Power requirements.

#### Dooms
When you roll triples experience the next of your three Dooms. Every apprentice knows that if they travel down the road of spellcasting long enough, their doom will eventually claim them. It waits at the end of every spellcaster's career.

Each school of magic has different Dooms.
1. The first time you roll triples, you suffer your first Doom.
2. The second time you roll triples, you suffer your second Doom.
3. The third time you roll triples, you suffer your third and final Doom.

While your first and second Dooms tend to be survivable, your final Doom usually spells the end of your career (usually through your death).

There are three ways to avoid your Doom.
1. Never use more than two Power at a time, forever limiting your magical power.
2. Seek out a solution to your Doom. These are always unique quests that your DM designs for you. They are usually pretty epic.
3. Sell your soul to a god or supernatural being.
