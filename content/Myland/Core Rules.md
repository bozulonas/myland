# House Rules

## Character Basics

### Attributes

Characters have three attributes: **Strength (STR)**, **Dexterity (DEX)**, and **Willpower (WIL)**. These are used to make [saves](#saves). 

### Saves

When attempting something risky, you might be asked to make a **save** to avoid a consequence. Roll a d20, if your attribute meets or beats the roll, you successfully avoid the consequence.
## Actions

When making a save you can...

### Leverage Time, Gear and Skill

Describe how you use the available time, gear and/or a relevant skill. This sets the save's [difficulty](#difficulty). 
- If you have all three it's trivial *(no roll needed)*, 
- Only two or one it's a challenge. 
- If you don't have any it may be impossible. 

A skill is something from your characters background.

> **Example**: You have a crowbar *(gear)*, but are short on time and skill. A save to avoid a threat while prying open a door is probably hard *(limited reward, severe consequences)*. However if you had ample time and skill it would be trivial *(no roll*).

### Raise the Stakes (aka Devils Bargain)

Describe how you adjust your approach to increase the reward, *(or gain advantage)* at the expense of greater risk.


## Core Mechanics

### Difficulty

As saves become easier or harder, the following can happen:

* **Risk and Reward**: What you stand to gain and lose from a save can change. 
	* Easier → reward increases, risk decreases. 
	* Harder → reward decreases, risk increases. 
* **Advantage/Disadvantage**: Roll twice and take the best/worst result.

### Time
Commonly measured in the following increments:
- **10sec**: Roughly how long it takes each participant to take an action in combat.
- **10min**: how long it takes for an action during exploration. One roll on the dungeon clock.
- **1hr**: Has elapsed whenever a 1 is rolled on the dungeon clock.

## Combat

### Attacks

**Attacks always hit**. Roll your weapon's die and do that much damage to an opponent, minus their armour. 

**Single highest die**. When multiple combatants or weapons attack the same target, it takes the single highest die as damage, per round.

**Impair**. When an attack is **impaired**, such is firing into cover, or fighting while grappled, roll d4 for damage regardless of weapon. 

**Bonus**: Under certain circumstances an attack receives a bonus, noted as +d6, +d8 etc. This is rolled as an extra attack against the same target. Remember that only the highest single die causes DAMAGE. Attacks against a completely helpless target always receive +d12.

**Blast** or **Cleave** attacks affects all targets in a specific area.

#### Maneuvers

Instead of your attack you may trip, shove, blind, grapple, bite, [impair](#attacks), or anything else you can imagine. 

The side most at risk makes a save. Instigators without an advantage tend to be at the most risk.

Maneuvers can be anything, the severity of the risk will be proportional to the impact of the maneuver, your skill, and any applicable gear.

### Hit Protection and Damage

Damage is dealt first to a creature's Hit Protection (HP). This represents avoiding or shrugging off real damage. Once HP is depleted, damage is dealt to STR.

If damage is by surprise, or self-Inflicted, it bypasses HP.

After taking STR damage, make a STR save to avoid critical damage.

When a creature takes critical damage, they take an Injured [Condition](#conditions), and are incapacitated until tended to by an ally and take a short rest.

If an incapacitated creature is left untended for 1 hour, they die.

#### Stress & Intoxication

Stress and Intoxication work just like damage, but after depleting HP they affect WIL and DEX respectively.

### Healing & Recovery

**Hit Protection** *(HP)* fully restores after a 10 min rest.

**Damage** *(STR)* restores 1 point per day of rest in a safe place *(town)*.

**Intoxication** *(DEX)* often fully restores after a nights rest *(depending on the intoxicant)*.

**Stress** *(WIL)* fully recovers when you [carouse](#carousing) or spend 30g indulging some vice.

Recovery can be accelerated by magic, items, NPCs and similar.

### Armor

Subtract your **Armor** value from any incoming damage. The maximum base Armor value is 3. Shields provide +1 Armor but require a free hand.

### Attribute score loss and death

If a creature has their **STR** reduced to zero, they are **dead**. 

If **DEX** is reduced to zero, they are **paralyzed**. 

If **WIL** is reduced to zero, they are **comatose**.

When your character **dies**, roll up a new one. The GM should introduce them as soon as possible. Getting back into the game quickly is more important than realism.

## Conditions

Conditions are negative effects. Each **Condition** must be placed in an inventory slot. 

Some Conditions have additional effects, which apply as long as the Condition remains in your inventory.

Conditions can only be removed by meeting their clear requirement—often a short, long or full rest.

Some common conditions are:
### Fatigue
**Fatigue** is a common one. It's cleared with a nights sleep, but otherwise has no effect other than occupying your inventory.

### Momentary Fatigue
Like Fatigue, but clears when you have a 10min rest (like hp)

## Deprivation

Lacking essentials like food or rest makes a character **Deprived**. A Deprived character cannot recover HP or attribute points. For each day they remain Deprived, they gain the **Fatigued** condition.

### Morale

NPCs and hirelings must make a **WIL save** to avoid fleeing. This check is triggered after they suffer their first casualty or when their group is reduced to half its original numbers. This rule does not apply to Player Characters.

## Growth & Advancement

### Hirelings

Hirelings are NPCs recruited to join the party. They can be created by rolling their attributes or by choosing a pre-made background that defines their skills and stats.

### Earning XP

Characters gain XP from:
1. Valuable treasures looted
2. Achievements earned 
3. Carousing when in civilization 

XP awards don't need to reflect monetary value; achievements or fabled items have intangible worth. Each PC gets the full XP value of each treasure.

| Quality | XP | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Poor | 0 | Bag of silver, used dagger, knucklebone dice |
| Normal | 1 | Bag of gold, gem, fine armor, magic scroll |
| Fabulous | 3 | Magic sword, giant diamond, mithral chainmail |
| Legendary | 10 | The Staff of Ord, a djinni's wish, a dragon hoard |

### Advancement

#### Class Levels
- XP Cost: [new level] x 5 XP
- Attribute increase: roll d20 once for each of your mouse’s STR, DEX and WIL. If a result is higher than the attribute’s current value, increase it by one. 
- Hit Protection Increase: Roll your class's hit points die and add it to your maximum HP
- Talent: Gain any talents listed on your class.

Notes:
- You can can 4 class levels max
- You can mix and match classes

### Carousing
When you return to civilization, you can carouse to celebrate your heroic exploits.

To carouse, each participant pitches in for the cost of the event. Then, each participant rolls 1d8 + the event's bonus to determine their own outcome.

Each character gains the XP noted for their result along with any other ill or positive effects.
If the group decides to play out the results of an individual roll, the entire carousing group is usually present for the events.

### Magic Items
- Certain wands, staves, relics and artifacts have magic trapped in them.
- Each such item will have a number of charges *(often 3)*
- To cast the magic held within an item, use an action and invest as many charges into it as you dare, up to the number of charges remaining.
- What happens:
	- **Spell effect** is based on the [sum] of the roll and the number of [dice] rolled.
	- **4 & 5 Fatigue**. For each die which shows 4 or 5 consume a charge.
	- **6 Explodes**. For each die which shows 6 roll another d6.
	- **Doubles** cause a [mishap](vancian%20magic#mishaps). 
	- **Triples** cause a [Doom](vancian%20magic#dooms)
#### Recharging items
When an items charges are expended, it is depleted and cannot be used. Each item has a recharge requirement. Fulfilling this requirement will coax the spirit back into the item and refresh all of its charges.
