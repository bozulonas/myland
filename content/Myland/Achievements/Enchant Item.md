Quest for extravagant ingredients

> For the GM: *Tome of Adventure Design (Revised) Table 1-3: Types of Missions, Page 28*

Cast a chosen spell into an item using 4 power.

That item becomes enchanted with the spell cast, containing 3 charges. [Magic Items](core%20rules#magic+items)

Make a WIL save to avoid a [spelling error](vancian%20magic#spelling+error).
