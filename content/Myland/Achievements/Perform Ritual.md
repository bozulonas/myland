When you draw on a place of power, obtuse ceremony, sacrifice,  the GM tells you:
- It’s going to take days/weeks/months.
- First you must                       .
- You’ll need help from                       .
- It will require a lot of money
- The best you can do is a lesser version, unreliable and limited
- You and your allies will risk danger from                       .
- You’ll have to disenchant                        to do it.

This let's you:
- Add power to a spell
- Prepare wards, each of which can be used to cancel a Power after it's rolled. (E.g. if you roll 6,6,6 you can expend a ward to remove a 6, reducing [sum] and avoiding the Doom)
