
[Gain a class level](Core%20Rules.md#Advancement#Class+Levels) by spending 5x [new level] XP. 
You can take the same class more than once!

## Alchemist
- Receive training in [[Alchemy]]
- Gain d6 hp.

## Assassin
- Choose one:
	- **Compromising Secrets**: Anything you can tie back to a comprising secret you know about a target becomes easier. In combat they grant +d8 damage. Each secret can be used once.
	- **Dodge**: Impair a d8 or d10 attack against you. You must be equipped with no more than light armor (1 Armor) and a d6 weapon.
- Gain d6 hp.

## Barber-Surgeon 
- *You a healer that deals in flesh and bone. Your craft is learned through experimentation and brutal failure. For many, you are the last face they see before the darkness takes them, and you are here to guide them towards a long life or a quick death.*
- Through trial and error, you've found these work well for you:
	- **Bone-saw:** Restore d6 STR to an ally. DEX save or the patient gains a permanent, ugly [condition](Core%20Rules#Conditions) reflecting the brutal surgery *(e.g., Horrific Scar, Gnarled Limb, Unsettling Twitch)*.
	- **Leeches:** Restore d6 DEX to an ally. DEX save or the patient takes d6 WIL damage.
	- **Laudanum**: Restore d6 WIL to an ally. DEX save or the procedure is mentally scarring. The patient gains a new psychological Condition *(e.g., Lasting Trauma, The Shakes, Troubling Phobia).*
	- **Triage**: Remove a Fatigued, Injured, or similar physical Condition from an ally. DEX save or your methods are brutal. The Condition is removed, but the patient takes 1 point of STR damage from the ordeal.
- Gain d6 hp.
## Bard
- Gain +1 to carousing rolls 
	- *(a d8 roll during downtime to determine how much XP is earned)*
- Gain +1 to reaction rolls 
	- *(a 2d6 roll the GM makes in the dungeon to decide if something want to kill you or not)*
- Talking you way into bed with NPCs is [easy](Core%20Rules.md#Difficulty)
- Gain d6 hp.

## Beastmaster
- You gain a loyal & obedient animal best friend. They have their own stats and act on their own in combat.
- Gain d6 hp.

## Berserker
- After taking Damage in combat, Raise your melee Damage dice and gain Advantage on the next Save against Critical Damage till the end of the next turn.
- Gain d8 hp.

## Brawler
- While not wearing any armour, your Armour score is +1. Gain bonus d4 unarmed Damage die (increasing in size each time you take this)
- Gain d8 hp.

## Duelist
Choose one:
	- **Duel**: Once per combat, until it ends, you can concentrate on fighting a single adjacent opponent, Enhancing your melee attacks against them and Impairing their melee attacks against anyone but you. All other enemies’ attacks against you are Enhanced.
	- **Parry**: Roll your weapon's damage die versus the incoming melee attack's damage die *(after the roll)*.
	    * **If you roll higher**: You nullify the attack and deal your roll as damage to the attacker, bypassing their armor.
	    * **If you tie**: Your weapons clang off each other, and the attack is nullified.
	    * **If you roll lower**: You take the attacker's roll as damage, bypassing your armor.
- Gain d8 hp.

## Knight
- Your trained in the following:
	- **Block**: [Impair](Core%20Rules.md#attacks) any d6 or d8 attack. You must be wielding a shield.
	- **Shield Smash:** When attacking, wielding a shield adds +d4 to the attack.
- Gain d8 hp.

## Priest
* Priests serve gods with selfish goals, locked in the power games of the eternal struggle.
* **Worship**: Make a worth **[sacrifice](Core%20Rules.md#sacrifice)** to a god to gain their blessing *(a minor ability)* and an **[invocation](Core%20Rules.md#invocation)** of their choosing *(you can bargain for a specific one by fulfilling their demands)*
* The maximum debt you can incur during an invocation is increased by +1.
- [[Gods]]
- Gain d6 hp.

## Sharpshooter
- After a ranged attack, gain bonus weapon Damage die with this weapon on the same target until you attack another target or the combat ends.
- Gain d6 hp.

## Spellcaster
- Roll for 10 [magical styles](https://www.seventhsanctum.com/generate.php?Genname=magicstyle) and choose one. The GM determines it's mechanics.
- Describe the arduous lengths you go to unlock this power.
- Gain d4 hp.

## Thief
- Choose One:
	- **Sleight of hand**: Whenever someone tries to retrieve or draw an item, you can reveal that you actually burgled it from them at some point in the recent past. Take 1 [fatigue](Core%20Rules.md#conditions)
	- **Unlabeled Package**: In town, you can collect two **Unlabeled Packages** from a contact which, when unwrapped, contain any common item you declare. 
		- *If you take this again, you can collect 4 instead of two*  
		- *You can choose to reveal that two unlabelled packages are, in fact, one bulky item*
- Gain d6 hp.

## Warlord
- You have mastered the following feats:
	- **Cleave**: Your attack gains +d12 or hits all adjacent foes. STR save or become [momentarily fatigued](Core%20Rules.md#momentary+fatigue).
	- **Exploit**: Alongside an attack you can perform a [maneuver](Core%20Rules.md#maneuver). DEX save or become [momentarily fatigued](Core%20Rules.md#momentary+fatigue).
	- **Deny**: Cancel any attack die *(after the roll)* aimed at you or an ally within reach . WIL save or become [momentarily fatigued](Core%20Rules.md#momentary+fatigue). 
- You cannot use your feats while you are fatigued *(momentarily or otherwise)*
	- *Design note: You can use these until you fail a save, each combat.*
- You can only use one feat per attack.
- Gain a d8 hp.

## Warrior
- Choose One:
	- **Mighty Deeds**: You can attack and perform [maneuvers](Core%20Rules#maneuvers) at the same.
	- **Mightier Deeds**: [Maneuvers](Core%20Rules#maneuvers) are easier. 
- Gain d8 hp

Below is a rough guide of how maneuvers change as they get easier.

| Examples                 | Default         | Easy            | Very Easy  | Trivial |
| ------------------------ | --------------- | --------------- | ---------- | ------- |
| Trip, Disarm, Blind      | Risky Save      | No Save         | No Save    | No Save |
| Cripple, Sunder, Concuss | Very Risky Save | Risky Save      | No Save    | No Save |
| Bisect, Obliterate       | Impossible      | Very Risky Save | Risky Save | No Save |

## Wizard 
- Learn spells by choosing one:
	- **Apprentice**: a draconian master instructs on the knowledge within a random [spellbook](https://saltygoo.github.io/class/wizard) *(a minor ability)* and two of its spells *(random)*.
	- **Collegian**: Attend a grueling [wizard college](https://crateredland.blogspot.com/2021/06/wizard-colleges-imply-wizard.html) to learn the college's cantrips and two of its spells at random. *(Rule changes may apply)*
	- **Inventor**: [Invent two new spells](Spell%20Invention) from time spent sequestered in your study.
- **Memorization**: You can study spellbooks, so you don't need to recite spells from the page. *(No save needed casting under time pressure)*
- **Ritualist**: Performing [Rituals](Sorcery#Ritual) becomes easier *(less conditions for a given effect)*
- See [[Sorcery]]
- Gain d4 hp.
